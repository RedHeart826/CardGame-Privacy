# Privacy Policy — Hokm and Pasoor

**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

## Overview
Hokm and Pasoor ("the App", formerly Reza's Cards and Reza's Suite), published by RedHeart, is a card game app featuring Pasoor and Hokm. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection
The App does **not** collect any personal data. We do not run any servers, and no information about you or your games is ever sent to us. No accounts are required to use the App. Three optional features share a little information with others, never with us: Play Nearby with the other devices at your table, and Game Center and online play with Apple. All are described below.

## Data Storage
All game progress, settings, statistics and your Play Nearby name are stored **locally on your device** using standard iOS storage. Deleting the App deletes them.

## Play Nearby
From version 3.0 the App lets people in the same room play together on their own iPhones and iPads. This uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity, which connects nearby devices directly over Wi-Fi, peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. It does not use the internet, and nothing goes through us.

When you use Play Nearby:
- **What is shared, and with whom.** Only with the other devices at your table: the name you type (any name or nickname you like), a random player identifier created by the App on your device, and your moves. While you host a table, its code and your name are visible to nearby devices that are running the App and looking for a table.
- **What is not shared.** Nobody receives your cards except you. Each player is sent only their own hand and the cards played on the table.
- **Security.** The connection between devices is encrypted.
- **Nothing is kept by us.** When the match ends or you leave, the connection closes. We never receive any of this information.
- **Permission.** iOS asks for permission to use the local network the first time you open Play Nearby. You can turn it off at any time in the iPhone or iPad Settings app, under Hokm Pasoor (the App's name on the Home Screen). Everything else in the App works without it.

## Game Center (optional)
From version 3.0 the App has leaderboards and achievements through Apple's Game Center. The App does
not use Game Center at all until you tap the Leaderboards (trophy) button or choose to play online. From then on, each time the
App opens it signs in to Game Center quietly, if you are signed in to Game Center on your device, and it
never shows Apple's sign-in screen unless you tap Leaderboards or choose to play online. While signed in, the App sends your scores on the leaderboards (matches won against the
computer, best streak and best round, Kots) and your achievements to Apple's Game Center, where
they are shown to you and to other Game Center players under your Game Center name. This is
handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive it. You can sign out at any time
in the Settings app under Game Center.

## Online play (from version 4.0)
From version 4.0 you can play Hokm and Pasoor online with friends or other players, through Apple's
Game Center. Online play is optional. The App does not start it until you choose it.

- **Signing in.** To play online you must be signed in to Game Center on your device. Your Game Center
  name, an identifier Apple gives this game, and a random identifier the App makes for itself are shown to, or sent to, the people you play with.
- **Tables with a code.** When you create a table, the App makes up a random four-character code.
  You decide whom to give it to. The App uses the code only to ask Game Center to bring together the
  players who enter it, and does not save it.
- **What is sent during a match.** The game's moves, your seat at the table, and the preset messages
  and stickers you choose are sent to the other players' devices **through Apple's Game Center
  servers**. There is no chat: you cannot type messages, only pick from a fixed list of phrases and
  stickers.
- **Your cards.** One player's device runs each match (the host), so it holds every hand, and a copy
  of the table goes to the player who would take over if the host leaves. The App shows each player
  only their own hand and the cards played on the table, and keeps the copy out of sight.
- **Leagues and wins.** Your league points and online wins are stored in Game Center leaderboards
  (Apple's service) and are shown to other Game Center players under your Game Center name.
- **Nothing reaches us.** We run no server and receive none of this information. Apple handles it under
  Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **On your device only.** The list of your recent online matches is kept only on your device. Deleting
  the App deletes it.
- **Signing out.** You can sign out of Game Center at any time in the Settings app, under Game Center.
  Everything else in the App works without it.

## Analytics & Tracking
The App does **not** use any analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

## Third-Party Services
The App does **not** integrate with any third-party data services. Play Nearby uses Apple's built-in Multipeer Connectivity framework, which runs on your device; leaderboards and online play use Apple's Game Center, only if you sign in.

## Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. In Play Nearby, the name a player types is shown only to the other players at the same table, who are physically nearby. Online play has no chat and no typed messages, only fixed preset phrases and stickers, and it uses players' Game Center names.
