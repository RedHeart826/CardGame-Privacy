## Description

Play Pasoor and Hokm, two classic Persian card games, on your iPhone or iPad. No account, no internet, no ads: just a deck, a table and a clever opponent.

PASOOR (also spelled Pasur)
The traditional fishing game. Match cards on the table that add up to eleven, sweep the table for a Sur, and race to the target score. Jacks sweep, Queens and Kings capture their own rank, and the 10 of Diamonds and 2 of Clubs are worth fighting for.

HOKM (also spelled Hokum)
The beloved partnership trick-taking game. Four players, two teams, one trump suit. As Hakem you choose trumps from your first five cards, then team up with your partner to take seven tricks. Win seven to 0 for a Kot, and watch out for the Hakem-Kot.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
- Learn as you play: each game has a clear how-to-play page
- Three difficulty levels, from relaxed to brutal
- Opponents that read the table and play like people, not scripts
- Hand-painted card faces and four card-back designs
- Choose your table color
- Original music, with a simple on/off and track picker
- Your game saves automatically and resumes where you left off
- Statistics for every match you play
- Built for iPhone and iPad, in portrait or landscape on iPad
- Private by design: no accounts, no tracking, no data collected

Whether you grew up playing Pasoor and Hokm at family gatherings or you are meeting them for the first time, if you enjoy trick-taking and card-matching games such as Spades, Whist, Bridge, Hearts or Tarneeb, you will feel at home.
